## Limitations

- The cohort is designed, not sampled: rates in it say nothing about real-world prevalence, sensitivity or specificity.
- The research profile of each archetype is the cohort designer's reading of the research report, not a diagnosis and not ground truth.
- Agreement with intended outcomes shows that the code applies ruleset 1.0.0 as written. It is not evidence of clinical accuracy.
- The ruleset is sex-blind and age-blind, so differences by sex or age band come only from the presentations the cohort assigns to them; the cohort assigns every archetype to every sex and age band equally.
- The sex-balance reweighting uses an illustrative presentation mix, not an estimate.
- Coded data only: free-text notes are not read, and a missing code is weak evidence of absence. Real records are messier than these.
- The ruleset is not clinically validated.
